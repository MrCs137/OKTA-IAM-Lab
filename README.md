## Overview

Identity and access management lab in Okta for **Zenvara Labs**, a fictional company I created so my labs share one consistent environment. The lab covers building a test directory, organizing users into department groups, and configuring single sign-on (SSO).

**Why it matters:** Group-based access and SSO are the foundation of role-based access control (RBAC). They rest on the same least-privilege principles I applied for three years managing access to a DoD health database.
